# TAD Bot — Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** September 12, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how TAD Bot handles information when you use the bot on Discord. ### 1. Information We May Process TAD Bot may process basic Discord information necessary for its features to work, such as: * Discord user ID * Discord username or display name * Server ID * Channel ID * Poll and voting information * Information included in commands or interactions with the bot TAD Bot does not intentionally request or collect passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information. ### 2. How Information Is Used Information processed by TAD Bot may be used to: * Create and manage polls. * Record or display votes when required for the bot's functionality. * Restrict certain features to authorized users or moderators. * Respond to commands and interactions. * Maintain and improve the bot. ### 3. Voting Information Depending on how a poll is configured, voting information may be visible only to authorized moderators or may be used internally to calculate poll results. TAD Bot does not sell or share voting information for advertising purposes. ### 4. Data Storage TAD Bot only stores information that is necessary for its features and operation. If information is stored, it may remain stored for as long as necessary to provide the bot's functionality or until it is deleted by the bot owner. ### 5. Third-Party Services TAD Bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. TAD Bot is not responsible for information collected or processed directly by Discord or other third-party services. ### 6. Data Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by TAD Bot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. ### 7. Children's Privacy TAD Bot is intended to be used through Discord and does not knowingly collect personal information from children independently of Discord. ### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any updated version will replace the previous version once published. ### 9. Contact For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or TAD Bot's handling of information, please contact the bot owner through the Discord server where TAD Bot is being used. By using TAD Bot, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
